Ace Combat

Ace Combat both a basic game about flying fighter jets, and a love letter to those machines and aviation. Many iconic airplanes are lovingly included and modeled in each game, such as the F-16 Fighting Falcon, the F-14 Tomcat (The iconic plane from Top Gun), and both the F-22 Raptor and F-35. However, the selection of aircraft is not limited to American planes. A long list of Russian aircraft are available, such as the MiG and Sukhoi series of jets, the Swedish Gripen, and the British Typhoon. On top of all these jets, the series also introduces its own planes, most of which are incredibly futuristic and, frankly, straight out of science fiction. But one thing is clear, the developers and the long-time fans of the series love aviation and all that comes with it.

Ace Combat

The gameplay loop is generally simple. Fly to an area, destroy ground and air targets, then rinse and repeat. Outside of that loop, the game provides the player with interesting stories, memorable characters, and notable boss fights. Some stories have you stuck in perpetual showdowns with a rival pilot from a hostile country. Sometimes those hostile countries have access to super weapons, such as orbital cannons or massive airborne carriers. Some missions make you believe you've accomplished your goal, only to introduce an entirely new squadron of enemy fighters. Others will put your wingman and squadmates in mortal danger, increasing the stakes and requiring the player's timely intervention. Features that seem mundane and simple have been mixed with inriguing story beats to give the players an almost "Hollywood-like" gameplay experience.

Ace Combat

What makes these games so popular? For one, the act of flying itself is very stylized, although not truly realistic. Players can use both realistic and futuristic weapon systems to decimate their targets. Players can use high-g turns to maneuver their aircraft into favorable firing positions. In the real world, most of these aircraft would have their wings ripped off by these maneuvers (minus some of the newer generation, hyper maneuverable, jets). The stories in each game are beloved, for the most part at least. Like any franchise, there are some outliers in narratives and gameplay. There aren't many games that match Ace Combat's gameplay or stories. Project Wingman is one alternative, however it doesn't have nearly the same size of fanbase as AC.

Ace Combat

So, where is the franchise headed? Ace Combat 8's October release showed incredible promise, setting a record as the fastest-selling entry in the series. The previous entry, Ace Combat 7, holds the record as the most sold entry in the series at around 7.5 million copies. The fanbase was not optimistic about getting another game after Ace Combat 7, despite its success. However, the new title's sales performance has proven that there is still a massive amount of interest in the series and fans will support the studio if development continues. I know that I personally will.
